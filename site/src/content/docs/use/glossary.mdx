---
title: Glossary
description: Every term a recruiter or manager meets on Octagon Analytics, alphabetically, with a link to where it is explained.
---

For anyone who meets a term in Claude's answers, the dashboard or the Coach and wants to know what it means. Terms are alphabetical; each links to the page that explains it in full.

## A

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Actor | The person who made a stage move. Funnel activity is credited to them, not the job owner | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#attribution) |
| Action queue | Your ranked calls for today, up to 10, from `/queue` | [Your day](/use/your-day/#queue-todays-calls) |
| Aftercare | Check-in calls on placements: before start, week 1, month 1, month 3. Works from Won deals' start dates | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#desk-and-client-lists) |
| Aging offer | Open role, offer out 5 to 60 days with no movement | [Your day](/use/your-day/#myday-the-standup) |
| Auto mode | A Cowork setting that stops you approving every step. Changes to RecruitCRM still wait for your confirm | [Getting started](/use/getting-started/#get-connected) |
| Away | The Coach's "pick your last day off" button. Pauses the Coach until that date | [Your day](/use/your-day/#the-buttons) |

## B

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| BD call | A Devyce call tagged `Contact - Prospect (BD)`. Weekly minimum 5 | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#how-calls-are-counted) |
| Billing | The value of Won deals with a close date in the quarter, credited to the deal owner. Not cash, not Placed | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#money) |
| Billing target | Your quarterly target. Anyone without one shows as TBC | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#quarterly-billing) |

## C

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Card nonce | The one-time code that ties your confirm to one preview. Can't be reused or made up | [Making changes](/use/making-changes/#the-confirm-card-in-plain-words) |
| Client Briefing Call | A date recorded on a job with its DPE | [Glossary: DPE](#d) |
| Client call | A Devyce call tagged `Contact - Client` or `Contact - Client Info`. Weekly minimum 5 | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#how-calls-are-counted) |
| Client rejection rate | Rejected by client ÷ CVs sent | [The funnel](/use/funnel/) |
| Client visit | A RecruitCRM meeting typed Client Meeting. Minimum 2 per consultant per quarter | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#client-visits) |
| Closure reason | Why a job closed or a deal was lost, offered when you make the change. Optional | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#logged-activity) |
| Coach | The Octagon Coach, a Slack app that DMs a pilot group named suggestions at 12:30, 16:45 and Monday 08:45 | [Your day](/use/your-day/#the-octagon-coach-in-slack) |
| Cold role | Open role with no candidate activity for 14 days (7 days in `/coldjobs` and `/chase`) | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#desk-and-client-lists) |
| Company Status | A company's BD stage in RecruitCRM, such as Prospect, Engaged, Client or Passive | [Asking questions](/use/asking-questions/#jobs-and-clients) |
| Connector | Octagon Analytics in Claude: what lets Claude read the figures and make changes | [Getting started](/use/getting-started/) |
| Conversion rate | A share of CVs sent, to 1 dp, always naming the ratio (CV→placed) | [The funnel](/use/funnel/#the-named-ratios) |
| CV Sent | The first funnel stage, and the CV sends KPI. Weekly minimum 10 | [The funnel](/use/funnel/) |

## D

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Deal owner | The person credited with a deal's billing, fees and aftercare | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#attribution) |
| Default window | 2026 year to date, end date exclusive, unless you name another | [Asking questions](/use/asking-questions/#how-to-ask) |
| Devyce | The call system whose call logs land in RecruitCRM. Only tagged Devyce calls count toward call KPIs | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#how-calls-are-counted) |
| DPE | A job's six scores, 1 to 5: Urgency, Track record, Process quality, Cooperation, Commitment, Positioning. They add up to the Total Score, which suggests a Job Grade. Recorded with `/dpe`, which also drafts the DPE email. What the letters stand for is not documented yet | [Commands](/use/commands/#dpe) |
| DPE email | Octagon's fixed-template email to the client after a DPE. Never includes the scores or grade. Saved to Outlook Drafts, and sets DPE Sent | [Your inbox](/use/inbox/#other-drafts-that-need-a-connected-inbox) |
| DPE Sent | The date the DPE email went, recorded on the job | [Commands](/use/commands/#dpe) |
| Draft | Every email Claude writes is a draft by default. Replies, Push & Tell, DPE and `/eow` emails are only ever drafts | [Making changes](/use/making-changes/#emails-are-drafts) |

## E

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| EOW | The end-of-week team email: the week's numbers, shout-outs, team table, offers and new jobs, drafted for a manager with `/eow`, and sent automatically to Dale every Friday at 16:30 to comment on and forward | [Commands](/use/commands/#eow) |
| Exceptions | People with something to act on: off plan, backlog, falling ratios or behind on visits. Managers only, `/exceptions` | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#for-managers) |

## F

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Feedback due | An interview held with nothing logged since. Silence in the system, not proof feedback wasn't given | [Your day](/use/your-day/#myday-the-standup) |
| Fee | The Won deal value. There is no separate fee record | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#money) |
| Fee percentage | Quote the median. A few mistyped values above 100 distort the mean | [Data caveats](/use/data-caveats/#deals-and-fees) |
| Findability | An optional 1 to 3 score on a DPE, not part of the Total Score | [Commands](/use/commands/#dpe) |
| Forecast | The forecast fee across open roles | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#money) |
| Formatted CV | Octagon's coversheet plus a redacted, reformatted CV for a client. `/formattedcv` | [Commands](/use/commands/#formattedcv) |
| Funnel | CV Sent → Interview Request → 1st → 2nd → 3rd → Offered → Placed | [The funnel](/use/funnel/) |

## H

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Handover | Moving a consultant's Open and On Hold jobs to someone else, for leavers, long leave or a desk change. Managers and admins | [Commands](/use/commands/#handover) |
| Hotlist | A shared talent pool of candidates the team keeps coming back to. `/hotlist` | [Commands](/use/commands/#hotlist) |
| Hygiene | Record gaps that make fee, billing, desk and call figures wrong, with the fix. `/hygiene` | [Data caveats](/use/data-caveats/#deals-and-fees) |

## I

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Inbox category | How an inbox email is sorted: routine, never, other or automated | [Your inbox](/use/inbox/#how-emails-are-sorted) |
| Intake | Octagon's candidate intake template, filled from your last Devyce AI call summary. `/intake` | [Commands](/use/commands/#intake) |
| Internal interview | A note typed `Candidate - Internal Interview`. Counted by `/noteskpi` | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#other-things-that-are-measured) |
| Internal JD | The Internal Job Description file attached to a job. `/jdcheck` finds open jobs without one Claude can read | [Commands](/use/commands/#jdcheck) |
| Interview Request | The second funnel stage, and a weekly KPI (minimum 5) | [The funnel](/use/funnel/) |
| Interviews | The KPI counting moves to 1st Interview. Weekly minimum 4 | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#the-weekly-kpis) |

## J

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Job Grade | A, B or C on a job. The DPE Total Score suggests one (A 25–30, B 19–24, C under 19); the consultant decides. Push & Tell covers A and B grade jobs | [Commands](/use/commands/#dpe) |
| Job owner | Whoever owns the role now. Desk views are credited to them | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#attribution) |
| JOF | Job order form. Filled in with `/editjob`; counted as a note typed `Job Order Form Complete` | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#other-things-that-are-measured) |

## L

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Lapsed client | A company we have placed with, with no Open or On Hold job and no new job in 6 months | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#desk-and-client-lists) |
| Lead | A note typed `Lead`. Can't be typed as a lead through Claude; set the type in RecruitCRM | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#other-things-that-are-measured) |
| Leaver | A consultant who has left. Stays in totals, tagged "left"; nothing is reattributed | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#attribution) |
| Looks like BD / client | Untagged calls that were probably BD or client calls. Shown beside the KPI, never added to it | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#looks-like-counts) |

## M

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Manager | A token with team view. Sees the whole team on desk views and can run manager commands | [Asking questions](/use/asking-questions/#managers-and-admins) |
| Mirror | Octagon's copy of RecruitCRM, kept in sync. Never edited directly | [Architecture](/run/architecture/) |
| My day | Your attention list: interviews, feedback due, aging offers, stalled candidates, cold roles. `/myday`, and "your day" at chat start | [Your day](/use/your-day/) |

## O

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Off limit | A candidate who must not be approached, usually because recently placed. Never pitched, matched or emailed | [Making changes](/use/making-changes/#off-limit-candidates) |
| Offered | The stage before Placed. A deal at Offered is pipeline, never billing | [The funnel](/use/funnel/) |
| Opted out | A contact or candidate who has opted out of marketing. Claude never emails them | [Making changes](/use/making-changes/#emails-are-drafts) |

## P

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Pace | In the Coach: your plan × days so far ÷ 5 | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#your-weekly-plan) |
| PII | Personal data. Candidate names and notes are internal only, never bulk-exported | [Making changes](/use/making-changes/#personal-data) |
| Pipeline | Deals at Open through Offered. The forward indicator, never added to billing | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#money) |
| Pitch | A candidate pitched to a client contact, recorded with `/pitch` in RecruitCRM's pitch feature | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#other-things-that-are-measured) |
| Placed | The last funnel stage. Carries no fee: billing needs the deal moved to Won | [The funnel](/use/funnel/#placed-is-not-billed) |
| Placement fee | The one Won deal paired with a placement on the same job | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#money) |
| Plan | Your weekly CVs, interview requests and interviews, worked back from your billing target. Never below the minimums | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#your-weekly-plan) |
| Preview | What Claude shows before any change. Nothing changes until you confirm | [Making changes](/use/making-changes/) |
| Push & Tell | The weekly update email to each client with A and B grade jobs: what we did (push) and what we need (tell). Drafts only; stamps the Push & Tell Email date on each job. `/pushtell` | [Commands](/use/commands/#pushtell) |

## R

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Recorded (Claude) | A stage move made through Claude on a pair it did not source: admin saved, not revenue | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#claudes-part-in-deals) |
| Rejected by client | The client turned the candidate down | [The funnel](/use/funnel/) |
| Rejected by consultant | We screened them out ourselves, including declined offers | [The funnel](/use/funnel/) |

## S

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow mode | The Coach sending its DMs to an admin for checking instead of the recruiter | [Your day](/use/your-day/#the-octagon-coach-in-slack) |
| Shortlist | A RecruitCRM stage only some desks use. Not the top of the funnel and never a denominator | [The funnel](/use/funnel/#shortlist) |
| Silver medallist | A candidate who reached 2nd, 3rd interview or Offered and was then rejected, worth putting forward again. `/silver` | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#desk-and-client-lists) |
| Sourced (Claude) | A candidate added to the job through Claude, or suggested by `/match` and sent within 60 days | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#claudes-part-in-deals) |
| Spec pitch | A speculative pitch of a candidate to a client, anonymised until the client engages. `/specpitch` writes it; `/pitch` records it | [Asking questions](/use/asking-questions/#writing-content) |
| Stage count | Distinct candidate-job pairs at the last time they entered the stage, credited to the mover | [The funnel](/use/funnel/#how-a-stage-is-counted) |
| Stalled | Open role, candidate at CV Sent to Offered, no movement for 10 to 60 days | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#desk-and-client-lists) |

## T

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Tagged call | A Devyce call with a category. Only these count toward BD and client targets | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#how-calls-are-counted) |
| Tagging rate | Share of calls of 30 seconds or more that are tagged | [Data caveats](/use/data-caveats/#call-categorisation) |
| Today's share | In the Coach: your weekly plan ÷ 5, rounded up | [KPIs](/use/kpis-and-targets/#your-weekly-plan) |
| Token | Your personal access key for the connector. Scopes figures to your desk and attributes changes to you. Treat it like a password | [Getting started](/use/getting-started/#get-connected) |
| Total Score | The sum of a job's six DPE scores. Suggests the Job Grade | [Commands](/use/commands/#dpe) |

## U

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Unbilled placement | A placement with no Won deal on its job. Flagged after 14 days | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#money) |
| Untyped meeting | A meeting with no type. Can't count as a client visit until typed | [Data caveats](/use/data-caveats/#only-what-is-logged) |

## W

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Watchdog | Job board watchdogs: created for each new vacancy (step 6 of the vacancy checklist) and reviewed in the 09:00 block of the standard day | [Your day](/use/your-day/#the-vacancy-checklist) |
| Won | The deal stage that records billing. Moving a deal to Won with its value, salary, fee % and start date is `/won` | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#money) |
| Won later this quarter | Won deals with a close date after today but inside the quarter. Counted in quarter billing, shown separately | [Metrics](/use/metrics/#money) |

## Y

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| Your day | The brief attention list Claude gives at the start of a chat, after the dashboard | [Your day](/use/your-day/#your-day-at-the-start-of-a-chat) |
