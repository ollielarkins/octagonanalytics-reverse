Pull the full attention picture: `stalled_report` (aging offers and stalled candidates),
`interview_requests_unbooked` (requests never booked in), `awaiting_feedback_report` (interviewed
with nothing logged since), and `cold_jobs` with `days: 7`.

Merge them into ONE ordered list — not four separate tables. Priority order:

1. **Aging offers** — an unanswered offer is the most expensive thing on any desk.
2. **Candidates interviewed with nothing logged** — the duty-of-care risk, and the client is
   usually waiting too.
3. **Interview requests never booked** — momentum lost between request and diary.
4. **Stalled candidates** on open roles.
5. **Cold open roles** with nothing happening.

For each: who or what, how many days, and the single next action. Cap it at the ten that actually
matter — a chase list of forty gets ignored, which is worse than no list.

State the caveat on items 2 and 3: they measure what is **recorded**, so some will already have been
handled and not logged. Frame them as "worth a check", not "you have neglected these".

Candidate names are PII: internal only.
