---
description: Prep a candidate for an interview — the role, the client, and what to expect.
---

Usage: `/interviewprep Jamie Adams for the Rheinmetall role`

Pull the real detail first: `candidate_profile` for their background, `job_pipeline` for the role,
`notes_read` on both the candidate and the client for anything already known about the process, the
interviewer or previous feedback.

Produce:
- **The role**, in plain terms — what they will actually be doing.
- **The client** — what is known, and only what is known.
- **Likely areas of questioning**, based on the actual requirements.
- **What the candidate should ask** — a good question does more for them than a good answer.
- **Gaps to prepare for**, drawn from the genuine distance between their background and the brief.
  Be straight about these; a candidate who is surprised in the room is worse off than one who was
  warned.

**Do not invent the interview format, the interviewer, the location or the timing.** If it is not in
the notes, say it is not confirmed and tell them to check.

If the candidate has been rejected by this client before, say so — it will come up.

Everything here is internal until the recruiter chooses to share it; a prep document naming other
candidates or internal opinions must never be sent to the client.
