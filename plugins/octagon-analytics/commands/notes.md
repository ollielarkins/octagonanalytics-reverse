---
description: Read the notes already recorded against a candidate, job, company or contact.
---

Usage: `/notes Jamie Adams` — `/notes job 6011` — `/notes client Rheinmetall`

Call `notes_read` with `candidate`, `job` or `client`. Optionally narrow with `from` and `to`.

Present newest first: date (DD/MM/YYYY), author, note type where set, and the text. Do not
paraphrase the notes into a summary unless asked — the recruiter usually wants what was actually
written, and a summary quietly loses the detail that mattered.

If a note is long, show it in full rather than truncating; if there are many, show the most recent
and say how many more there are.

Two things worth knowing:
- Most notes are **untyped** — around 88% of recent ones. A missing type does not mean the note is
  unimportant, it means nobody set one.
- Around 84,000 notes are `Migrated` legacy records from 2018-2024, imported from the previous
  system. If you are showing old notes, say which are migrated so their age is not misread as
  recent activity.

Note text is PII and often candid: internal only, never client-facing.
