description: Your day, laid out on Octagon's standard structure with your real priorities slotted in.
---

Call `my_day` for live priorities (aging offers, stalled candidates, cold open roles) and
`weekly_kpis` for where they are behind this week. Add `cold_jobs` or `stalled_report` if more
detail is needed.

Then lay the day out on Octagon's structure, putting the REAL items into each block:

- 08:00-09:00 Call shortlists & start candidate search
- 09:00-10:00 Review plan/watchdogs/meetings; call new candidates; set up searches for focus jobs
- 10:00-11:00 Candidate time — call shortlists, prep/arrange interviews, LinkedIn
- 11:00-12:00 Business Development — call job & client lists; follow up pitched candidates
- 12:00-13:00 Lunch
- 13:00-14:00 Candidate time (shortlists + new candidates); watchdogs
- 14:00-16:00 Search & call candidates for the focus job
- 16:00-16:30 Admin — adverts, pitch candidates, leads, update deals
- 16:30+ Chase the strong candidates on your shortlists

**Name specific roles and candidates** in each block — a plan that says "make BD calls" is the
timetable, not a plan. Put aging offers and stalled candidates on open roles into the morning
candidate blocks, prospects into the BD hour.

Where they are behind a weekly KPI, add one concrete nudge with the number in it ("at 3 of 10 CV
sends — two out today would keep the week alive"). One nudge, not a list. Nudge, don't nag.

If `my_day` cannot resolve who is asking, say so and stop rather than planning a generic day.

Candidate names are PII: internal only.
