---
description: Why candidates fall out — client rejections versus our own screening.
---

Usage: `/rejections` — `/rejections Keelan Riley last quarter`

Call `rejection_report`. Defaults to 2026 YTD; pass `from`, `to`, `consultant` or `limit`.
State the window as DD/MM/YYYY.

Present the split:
- **Rejected — Client**: the client turned them down after we put them forward.
- **Rejected — Consultant**: we screened them out ourselves.

Then the breakdown by consultant and by client, highest first.

The ratio between the two is the useful signal. Heavy client rejection after CV send suggests the
brief is not understood or the bar is being misjudged; heavy consultant rejection is usually
healthy screening, unless volume is so high it means the sourcing is off target.

Firm-wide, consultant rejections outnumber client rejections several times over — that is normal
and expected. Do not present a high consultant-rejection count as a problem on its own.

This is diagnostic, not disciplinary. It answers "where is the process leaking", not "who is bad at
their job".

Candidate names are PII: internal only.
