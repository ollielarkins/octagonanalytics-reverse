Call `my_day` from the **octagon-analytics** connector. It scopes to whoever is asking, from their
token — no argument needed. Pass `consultant` only if someone explicitly asks about another person.

Present as a short standup, most urgent first:
- **Aging offers** — offer out with no response. These are the ones that go cold silently.
- **Stalled candidates** — active stage, no movement.
- **Cold open roles** — nothing happening on a live job.
- Anything from `weekly_kpis` they are behind on this week, with the gap as a number.

Keep it to what needs a decision today. Name the specific candidate or role and the single next
action for each. Do not list everything — a standup nobody finishes reading is a standup nobody uses.

If `my_day` cannot resolve the caller to a consultant, say so in one line and stop rather than
guessing whose desk to show.

Candidate names are PII: internal only.
