description: What is slipping firm-wide — aging offers and stalled candidates on open roles.
---

Call `stalled_report`. Defaults are sensible; pass `stall_days`, `offer_days` or `max_days` only if
asked to widen or narrow it.

Present two sections, most overdue first:
- **Aging offers** — offer out 5-60 days with no response. Candidate, job, consultant, days waiting.
- **Stalled candidates** — active stage, no movement 10-60 days. Same columns.

Both are limited to OPEN roles and a recent window on purpose. An earlier version of this report
returned 1,517 items, most over a thousand days old — candidates frozen at an interim stage years
ago and never marked rejected or placed. That is history, not a to-do list. If someone asks for
everything, you can widen `max_days`, but say plainly that the old ones are abandoned records
needing a tidy-up, not chases.

Owner-attributed — this answers "whose desk is this", not "who last touched it".

Lead with the oldest aging offer: an offer sitting unanswered is the most expensive thing on the
list. At most three next actions.

Candidate names are PII: internal only.
