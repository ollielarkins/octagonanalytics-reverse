description: The money behind Won deals — total and median fee, fee percentage achieved, by consultant.
---

Call `fee_analysis`. Defaults to 2026 YTD; pass `from`, `to`, `consultant` or `limit` if narrowed.
State the window as DD/MM/YYYY.

Present:
- **Headline**: total fee, median fee, and the fee percentage actually achieved (median plus
  quartiles) — the achieved percentage is the interesting number, not the total.
- **By consultant**, highest total first.

Money as £ with thousands separators; percentages to 1 dp.

Median matters more than mean here — a single large placement drags an average and makes a desk
look unlike itself. If you quote a mean, quote the median beside it.

Fee percentage achieved versus agreed is the thing worth noticing: a consistently low achieved
percentage is a negotiation pattern, not a data problem. Say what the number shows; the reading of
it belongs to the recruiter and their manager.
