---
description: Write a speculative pitch putting a candidate to a client — anonymised by default.
---

Usage: `/specpitch Jamie Adams to Rheinmetall`

**Step 1 — off-limit check first.** Call `off_limit` with `action: check`. If the candidate is off
limit, stop. Do not write the pitch.

**Step 2 — get the facts.** `candidate_profile` for the real detail, and `notes_read` for anything
recorded about what they want. Write from what is on file, not from what sounds good.

**Step 3 — write it anonymised.** No name, no current employer, nothing that identifies them —
until the client engages. Lead with the two or three things that make them worth a conversation:
the specific capability, the availability, the reason they are looking. Keep it under a screen.

**Never invent.** No salary, no notice period, no achievement, no qualification the profile does not
show. `[placeholders]` for gaps.

Subject line included.

This writes the pitch. It does not send it — `/email` does that, and it defaults to draft. Recording
the pitch against the client in RecruitCRM is `/pitch`, which is separate again and the step people
forget, so the KPI under-reports.
