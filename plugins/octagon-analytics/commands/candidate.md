---
description: The full picture on one candidate — work history, education, every job and stage.
---

Usage: `/candidate Jamie Adams`

Call `candidate_profile` with the name or slug. For prior context — call notes, intake notes — also
call `notes_read` for that candidate, because the profile alone does not include them.

Present:
- Who they are: current role, employer, location, salary and expectation, notice.
- Work history and education.
- Every job they are on, with the current stage, in funnel order.
- Recent notes, newest first, if any.

Use this before writing a summary, prepping an interview or pitching someone, so the detail is real
rather than remembered. If a field is empty, say it is not recorded — do not fill the gap with a
plausible guess, and do not infer seniority, salary or availability from a job title.

If the candidate is **off limit**, say so at the top, before anything else.

Everything here is PII: internal only, never client-facing in this form, no bulk export. A
client-facing summary is a separate, anonymised piece of writing.
