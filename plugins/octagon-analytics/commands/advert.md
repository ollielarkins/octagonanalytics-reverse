description: Write a job advert from a role in RecruitCRM or from a brief you paste.
---

Usage: `/advert 6011` — `/advert` then paste the brief

If a job is named, call `job_pipeline` to pull the real title, client and salary, and `notes_read`
for any intake notes — an advert written from the actual brief beats one written from the title.

Write: a headline, a short hook, what the role is, what they need, what is on offer, and a clear
call to action.

**Never invent a fact.** No salary you were not given, no benefits, no client name, no location, no
hybrid arrangement. Use `[placeholders]` for every gap and list them at the end so the recruiter
knows exactly what to fill.

**Anonymise the client** unless the recruiter confirms the client is happy to be named. Most are
not, and an advert naming a client who did not agree is a real problem.

Inclusive language throughout: no age or gender coding, no "young and dynamic", no unnecessary
requirements that screen people out for no reason. Prefer "you will" to "the successful candidate
will". If the brief contains a requirement that reads as discriminatory, flag it rather than
polishing it.

Warm and professional. Short sentences. Written for a person, not a search engine.
