---
description: Pitch history — which candidates were pitched to which clients, and what happened.
---

Usage: `/pitches` — `/pitches Jamie Adams` — `/pitches Rheinmetall`

Call `pitch_history`. Narrow by candidate or client if named.

Present newest first: candidate, contact, client, pitch stage, date (DD/MM/YYYY). Group by client
when showing several.

Pitched candidates is a reported KPI and it only counts what was actually recorded through the
pitch feature. A recruiter who pitches constantly by phone and never records it shows as zero —
say so if the number looks implausibly low, rather than presenting it as the activity level.

Useful before pitching someone again: if a candidate has already gone to that client and been
passed over, pitching them again without acknowledging it is awkward for everyone.

To record a new pitch, use `/pitch`.

Candidate and contact names are PII: internal only.
