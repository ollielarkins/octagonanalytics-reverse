Call `billing`. It scopes to whoever is asking — a recruiter sees their own row, admins the team.
No arguments.

Present:
- **This quarter**: Won revenue against quarterly target, and the gap.
- **Pipeline in play** — the forward indicator, not billing.
- All-time Won for context.

Money as £ with thousands separators.

Say these plainly, every time:
- Billing is **Won deal value**. A candidate at the Placed stage carries no fee — the deal has to be
  moved to Won with a value entered, which is `/won`. This is the most common reason a desk feels
  ahead of what the number shows.
- Pipeline is not revenue. Never add it to Won or present a combined figure.
- Invoicing is not used in RecruitCRM at all, so nothing here reflects what has actually been
  invoiced or paid.

If someone is behind target, say by how much and name the nearest deal that would close the gap.
Nudge, don't nag — and do not moralise about the number.
